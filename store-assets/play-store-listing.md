# Play Store Listing — RAW@ Powerlifting Academy (RPA)

## Short description (80 char max)

## Full description (4000 char max)

RAW@ Powerlifting Academy puts a real coaching relationship in your pocket. Complete a full athlete intake, get a training program built specifically for you, log every set, and watch your numbers move — all in one app.

FOR ATHLETES
• Guided intake covering your training history, best lifts, recovery, technique, and medical background, so your coach programs around the real you
• Weekly training programs with sets, reps, target RPE, and coaching cues for every exercise
• Fast workout logging with a built-in rest timer
• Progress tracking with estimated 1-rep max charts for squat, bench, and deadlift, plus PR call-outs
• A searchable exercise library with technique cues and demo videos
• Membership management and payment history in one place

FOR COACHES
• A live roster of every athlete, with an at-a-glance status on who needs a new program this week
• Full visibility into each athlete's intake, training history, and logged workouts
• A program builder to assign a new week in minutes, with a "copy last week" shortcut
• Membership plan management and a transaction log across all athletes

Built for a real coach-athlete relationship, not a generic workout tracker.

## Data Safety section (Play Console) — what to declare
Data collected: Personal info (name, email, DOB, gender, city), Health & fitness (weight, body fat %, injuries, medical conditions, training data), Financial info (purchase history / amounts — not raw card/UPI details, which Razorpay handles), App activity (in-app actions, workout logs).

- Data is encrypted in transit: Yes
- Users can request data deletion: Yes (via the contact email in the Privacy Policy)
- Data shared with third parties: Razorpay (payment processing only), Supabase (infrastructure/processor, not for their own use)
- Data sold to third parties: No
- Data used for advertising: No
